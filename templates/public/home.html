01 / THE PLATFORM

A calmer way to prepare for a professional conversation.

Legal situations can be difficult to describe. IETA Legal gives you a structured place to record what happened, review your own information, and decide whether to approach an independently verified advocate.

The platform supports your preparation. It does not provide legal advice, represent you, select an advocate for you, or guarantee an outcome.

02 / A CLEARER PATH
01

Organise the facts

Build a factual intake with dates, locations, people, documents, and your own questions.

02

Use AURA carefully

AURA can ask concise factual questions and summarise what you provide. It is not a lawyer.

03

Choose your advocate

Explore transparent profile information and retain control over who you contact.

03 / THE BOUNDARY

AURA gathers information. Advocates provide professional advice.

Keeping those roles separate is intentional. AURA does not interpret law, recommend remedies, rank advocates, predict outcomes, or calculate deadlines.
